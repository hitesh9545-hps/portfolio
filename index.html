Face Mask Detection System

A real-time system that detects whether a person is wearing a mask or not using computer vision techniques.

How It Works:

Video frames captured via webcam are parsed for facial regions with OpenCV, then streamed into a TensorFlow/Keras neural classifier to detect mask compliance instantly.

Real-time detection with bounding box classification
Trained on public dataset using TensorFlow & Keras
Python OpenCV TensorFlow Keras
Automation & Hardware
Completed Project

Automatic Water System

An automatic water dispensing system designed for human use. When a glass is placed under the dispenser, an IR sensor detects the glass and the system automatically dispenses water.

How It Works:

IR sensor detects the presence of a glass. Once detected, the water pump is activated and water is dispensed automatically. Water stops when the glass is removed.

Zero-contact touchless dispensing for human hygiene
Integrated IR sensor, water pump, power supply, and control relay
IR Sensor Water Pump Control Circuit Power Supply
